# Brand Notes

Acento should feel calm, precise, premium, and culturally respectful.

## Visual Direction

- Warm paper backgrounds.
- Deep ink text.
- Sienna accents.
- Plantain and sea tones used sparingly.
- No cartoon characters.
- No noisy streak/gamification overload.

## Voice

- Clear.
- Respectful.
- Context-first.
- Never exoticizing.
- Never presenting slang as universally safe.
